## Florian Follonier

Applied AI architect and hands-on builder at Microsoft. I turn AI strategy into secure, operable systems.

I work on agentic applications, evaluation, observability, enterprise integration, and the engineering required to move from prototype to production. My recent projects also explore speech AI, embodied agents, and data-driven market research.
